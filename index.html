Escape Room

Ratio Station

6th grade ratios, rates, and percents

The supply vaults locked when the ratio logs scrambled. Each vault is a different door: Scale, Table, Percent, Crate, and Snack. Fix a vault and it drops that door's digits. Open every vault to clear the bay.

You have 45 minutes. Finish one vault, then open the next. Agree as a team before you lock an answer in.

How to play

  1. Open a vault. Read the supply problem.
  2. Solve it, then choose A-D.
  3. Four right answers reveal that vault's digits. A wrong set sends you back to fix the vault.
  4. Finish Scale Vault, Table Vault, Percent Vault, Crate Vault, then Snack Vault.
  5. The bay opens only if every vault's digits match.
