6th Grade Ratios, Rates, and Percents Escape Room

The supply vault locked when the snack and fuel logs scrambled. Repair a log to reveal its code. The door opens when the four codes are entered in order.

Work through all 5 rooms. Finish every challenge per room, then unlock to move on!

You have 45-60 minutes to escape. Read every answer choice before deciding!

How to play

  1. Read each challenge carefully.
  2. Discuss with your team and tap the best answer (A–D).
  3. Complete every challenge in the room — your choices are saved automatically.
  4. Open the room unlock page. If your answers are right, the room unlocks!
  5. If unlock fails, go back, revise answers, and try again.
