The supply vault locked when the ratio logs scrambled. Five logs are still open: snack scale, fuel table, percent quota, mixed crate, and the vault check. Fix a log and it drops that lock's digits. Enter every lock to open the door.

You have 45 minutes. Finish one log, then open the next. Agree as a team before you lock an answer in.

How to play

  1. Open a log. Read the supply problem.
  2. Solve it, then choose A-D.
  3. Four right answers reveal that lock's digits. A wrong set sends you back to fix the log.
  4. Finish Scale, Table, Percent, Crate, then Vault.
  5. The door opens only if every digit matches.
